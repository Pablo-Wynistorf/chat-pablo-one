# About Pablo Wynistorf

Pablo Wynistorf is an IT Technician from Switzerland who specialises in cloud computing
and network infrastructure. He builds projects primarily on Amazon Web Services (AWS) and
publishes a portfolio of his work and certifications at pablo.one.

## Cloud computing & competitions

- **WorldSkills Lyon 2024** — Pablo represented Switzerland in the Cloud Computing skill
  and was awarded a *Medallion for Excellence* (September 2024). The competition ran over
  three days and was carried out exclusively on the AWS platform. WorldSkills Lyon 2024 was
  the **47th WorldSkills Competition**, held at Eurexpo Lyon, France from 10–15 September
  2024 — the global championship of vocational skills, with competitors from 80+ countries.
- In Switzerland the Cloud Computing discipline runs under the **Skills53** banner
  (skills53.ch), the SwissSkills/WorldSkills cloud computing category for IT apprentices,
  with challenges built on AWS.
- His WorldSkills preparation began in January 2024 under his coach **Pascal Mathis**,
  and included extensive hands-on practice, studying a wide range of AWS services, and
  taking part in AWS Jam and AWS GameDay sessions and several demo competitions.
- **SwissSkills 2023** — Pablo competed in the Cloud Computing category and reached
  **second place**, which earned him an invitation to the selection assessment for the
  national WorldSkills team.

## AWS certifications

- **AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate** (achieved May 2023).
- **AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional** (achieved November 2024).

These certifications validate his skills in designing distributed systems on AWS.

## Event networking projects

Pablo has worked on-site building temporary network infrastructure for large events,
frequently together with the partner company **Konzept5**:

- **Openair Frauenfeld 2023 & 2024** — designed and installed the network for all food
  and beverage locations, deploying 50+ Cisco switches and around 30 access points, plus
  public WiFi (including a Swisscom stand), backstage connectivity, and CCTV transport.
- **UEFA Champions League Final 2023 (Istanbul)** — stadium network for photographers on
  the pitch, media tribune, VIP-tent public WiFi, and UEFA staff connectivity.
- **UEFA Conference League Final 2022 (Tirana)** and **2024 (Athens)** — comparable
  stadium networking for photographers, media, and UEFA employees.
- **UEFA Supercup 2023 (Athens)** — event network support.
